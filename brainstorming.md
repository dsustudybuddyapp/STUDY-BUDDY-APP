# AND102 | Intermediate Android Development GROUP CAPSTONE PROJECT IDEAS

**Connor Little:** Trivia Game, Airport Explorer, Study Buddy

## Step 1: Brainstorming Ideas

**Trivia Game:** A game where users answer multiple-choice questions to earn points. Users can choose different categories and try to beat their high scores!

**Airport Explorer:** An app where users can search for available flights and compare ticket prices. A stretch goal would be allowing users to look at hotels near their destination.

**[WINNER] Study Buddy:** An app that helps students stay organized by keeping track of assignments, upcoming deadlines, and study sessions. Users can add their own assignments and set when they're due.

## Step 2: Evaluating App Ideas

### 1. Trivia Game

- **Mobile:** Users can easily play trivia games on their phones using touch controls. I could also add sounds and notifications.
- **Story:** A fun way for users to test their knowledge and learn new things.
- **Market:** Students and people who enjoy trivia games.
- **Habit:** Users might come back to beat their high scores or try different categories.
- **Scope:** Relatively easy to build. I could use a free trivia API to get questions instead of making them myself.

### 2. Airport Explorer

- **Mobile:** Users can search for flights on their phones and potentially use location services to find nearby airports.
- **Story:** Helps people plan trips by finding flights and comparing prices in one place.
- **Market:** People who travel frequently or are planning vacations.
- **Habit:** Users would probably use the app whenever they're planning a trip rather than every day.
- **Scope:** More complicated because I would need an API that provides flight availability and prices. Some APIs might also cost money or have limitations.

### 3. Study Buddy

- **Mobile:** Users can manage assignments on their phones and receive notifications about upcoming deadlines. I could also add a study timer.
- **Story:** Helps students stay organized and avoid forgetting assignments or deadlines.
- **Market:** High school and college students who need help managing their schoolwork.
- **Habit:** Students could use the app daily to check assignments, add new tasks, and track their progress.
- **Scope:** Manageable to build using Android Studio. I can start with adding assignments, setting deadlines, and marking tasks as completed. More features can be added later.

## Step 3: Final Decision

**Final Idea: Study Buddy**

I decided to go with Study Buddy because it's useful for students and seems manageable to build within the time I have. It also gives me enough features to work on without making the project too complicated.

My main goal is to let users add assignments, set deadlines, and keep track of what they need to complete. If I have extra time, I could add features like study timers, reminders, and progress tracking.
